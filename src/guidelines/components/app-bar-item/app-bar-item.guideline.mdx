The App Bar Item is a circular icon that anchors a third-party app or plugin
in the right-side App Bar. Each item is a single tap-target with optional
selection styling and notification badges.

Use App Bar Items for plugin entry points that need persistent visibility —
GitHub, GitLab, Zoom, and similar integrations. Channel- and message-level
actions belong elsewhere.

<Divider />

## Anatomy

- <Num>1</Num> **Icon** — round 24px glyph or app logo. Custom artwork is
  cropped to a circle.
- <Num>2</Num> **Selection indicator** — a 3px vertical accent on the left
  edge when the app's panel is open.
- <Num>3</Num> **Mention badge** — numeric, shown for unread mentions.
- <Num>4</Num> **Unread badge** — a small dot, shown when there are unreads
  but no mentions.

## States

App Bar Items have two states. **Default** is the resting state. **Selected**
shows a left-edge accent that matches the active sidebar border, signalling
that the app's panel is open.

The mention and unread badges are mutually exclusive — when both apply, the
mention badge wins because it carries a count.
