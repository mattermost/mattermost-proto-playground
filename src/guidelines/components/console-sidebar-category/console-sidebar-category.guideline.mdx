Console Sidebar Category is the section header inside Console Sidebar. It
groups a set of `ConsoleSidebarItem`s under a single labeled banner.

Categories let administrators scan the sidebar for the section they want
without reading every item. They are visual anchors, not interactive — the
header itself does not navigate.

<Divider />

## Anatomy

- <Num>1</Num> **Icon** — a 16px compass icon that represents the category
  (e.g. credit-card-outline for Billing, shield-outline for Compliance).
- <Num>2</Num> **Label** — uppercase category name, sentence-case at the
  source and uppercased visually.

<Divider />

## Content

- Use short category labels — two or three words at most. They render
  uppercased and need to stay scannable.
- Pair every category with an icon. The icon is the primary affordance once
  administrators learn the layout.
- Avoid category names that overlap (e.g. don't pair "Authentication" with
  "Security" — fold one into the other).
