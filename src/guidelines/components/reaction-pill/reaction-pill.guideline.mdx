Reaction Pills surface lightweight, transient signals during a call —
emoji reactions, raised hands, and system notifications like "you've been
muted." They float over the call surface long enough to register and then
disappear; they aren't meant to be durable UI.

Reach for a Reaction Pill when something happens during a call that the
participant should notice without leaving their current view.

<Divider />

## Anatomy

Pills compose a leading symbol with a label, and — for system notifications —
a trailing dismiss icon.

<AnatomyStage style={{flexDirection:'column',gap:'0.75rem',alignItems:'flex-start'}}>
  <ReactionPill type="Reaction" emoji="🎉" label="Leonard R." />
  <ReactionPill type="Hand Raise" label="Leonard R." />
  <ReactionPill type="Other" message="You have been muted by the host" />
</AnatomyStage>

- <Num>1</Num> **Leading symbol** — an emoji (Reaction) or compass icon
  (Hand Raise, Other).
- <Num>2</Num> **Label** — the participant's name, the reaction text, or the
  system message.
- <Num>3</Num> **Trailing dismiss** — only on the Other (system) variant;
  closes the notification.

<Divider />

## Variants

| Variant   | Use when                                                                                  |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reaction  | A participant sends an emoji reaction. Show the emoji + their name.                       |
| Hand Raise | A participant raises their hand. Show the hand icon + "[Name] Raised Hand".              |
| Other     | A system notification (muted, removed, etc.). Show the system icon + a dismiss affordance. |

<Divider />

## Sizes

Three sizes match the call surface — **Small** for compact in-call rails,
**Medium** for the main stage overlay, **Large** for full-screen mobile.
