The Recording Pill is the persistent indicator that a call is being recorded.
It sits in the call surface, makes the recording state unmistakable, and
gives anyone with permission a single click to stop.

Privacy and consent depend on this pill being visible the entire time a
recording is in progress — never hide it, even on compact layouts.

<Divider />

## Anatomy

A leading status icon paired with the **REC** label, wrapped in a pressable
container.

<AnatomyStage style={{gap:'1rem',flexWrap:'wrap'}}>
  <RecordingPill state="Recording" />
</AnatomyStage>

- <Num>1</Num> **Status icon** — a spinner while initializing, a record glyph
  while recording, a stop glyph on hover.
- <Num>2</Num> **Label** — always **REC**. The label never changes; only the
  icon does.

<Divider />

## States

| State        | What it shows                                                                          |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Initializing | Spinner + REC. The recording is being set up; the pill is not yet actionable.          |
| Recording    | Record icon + REC. The default in-progress state.                                      |
| Hover        | Stop icon + REC + tooltip "Click to Stop". Reveals the stop affordance to authorised users. |

<div style={{display:'flex',gap:'1rem',flexWrap:'wrap',alignItems:'flex-start',padding:'1rem 0'}}>
  <RecordingPill state="Initializing" />
  <RecordingPill state="Recording" />
  <RecordingPill state="Hover" />
</div>

The hover state is only meaningful for users who can stop the recording.
Hide the hover affordance for participants who don't have permission — for
them, the pill stays in its Recording state and acts as a label, not a
button.
