Console Panel groups related settings or tabular data into a bordered card on
a System Console page. Most non-trivial console pages are a stack of these.

A panel is the unit of meaning in the console: one panel, one feature or one
table. Wrap content into multiple panels rather than letting a single panel
sprawl.

<Divider />

## Anatomy

A panel is a `ConsolePanelHeader` plus a body slot:

- <Num>1</Num> **Header** — title, optional subtitle, optional leading icon,
  optional tags ("Enterprise" / "Beta"), and an action cluster on the right
  (Switch, primary button, expand chevron). See Console Panel Header for
  details.
- <Num>2</Num> **Body** — any content. Console Setting rows, Console Property
  Tables, prose, or a custom layout.

<Divider />

## Behavior

When `expandable` is true, the chevron in the header toggles the body. The
panel is expanded by default. Use this for low-frequency settings that bulk
out the page without being relevant most visits — never for the main panel
on a page.

When not expandable, the chevron is hidden and the body is always rendered.

<Divider />

## Content

- Title should describe what the panel governs, in noun form ("Membership
  Policies", "Encryption Managers"). Skip verbs.
- Use the subtitle to clarify scope when the title alone could be misread.
  Keep it to one sentence.
- One primary action per panel. Multiple primary actions read as competing
  decisions; promote one and demote the rest to tertiary buttons in the body.
