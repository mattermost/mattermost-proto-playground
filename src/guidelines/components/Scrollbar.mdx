Scrollbars let people move through content that exceeds the viewport while
showing where they are in the larger whole. Compass uses a single, minimal
treatment — a transparent track with a thin, translucent thumb — so the
scrollbar reads as a position indicator first and a control second.

Per Jakob's Law, scrollbars don't get hover or active states; they should
behave the way scrollbars behave everywhere else.

## Anatomy

<div style={{display:'flex',gap:'1.5rem',flexWrap:'wrap',alignItems:'flex-start',padding:'1rem 0'}}>
  <div style={{height:'160px',display:'flex',alignItems:'stretch'}}>
    <Scrollbar orientation="Vertical" thumbSize="33%" scrollPosition={20} />
  </div>
  <div style={{width:'200px'}}>
    <Scrollbar orientation="Horizontal" thumbSize="33%" scrollPosition={20} />
  </div>
</div>

- <Num>1</Num> **Track** — the transparent area that contains the thumb. Click
  the track to jump-scroll.
- <Num>2</Num> **Thumb** — the visible indicator. Its length shows how much
  of the content fits in the viewport; its position shows where you are.
  Drag to reposition.

<Divider />

## Variants

| Variant     | Values                              | Use when                                                  |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Orientation | Vertical, Horizontal                | Match the scroll direction of the container.              |
| Thumb size  | 25%, 33%, 50%, 75% of the track     | Reflects the visible portion of the total content.        |
| Position    | 0–100% offset along the track       | Reflects the user's current scroll offset.                |

A larger thumb means more of the content is visible. When the thumb fills the
track at 100%, hide the scrollbar — there's nothing to scroll.

## Use the minimal-scrollbar treatment

Any element with `overflow: auto`, `overflow: scroll`, `overflow-y: auto|scroll`,
or `overflow-x: auto|scroll` should adopt the minimal scrollbar treatment.
Don't ship the browser-default chunky scrollbar on content regions.

The shared mixin lives at `src/styles/mixins.scss` and is auto-injected into
every module SCSS file:

```scss
.your-scroll-region {
  overflow-y: auto;
  @include minimal-scrollbar;
}
```

What the mixin gives you:

- A thin track (`scrollbar-width: thin` on Firefox; 8px width on WebKit).
- A transparent track and a translucent thumb that adapts to light and dark
  themes via `--center-channel-color-rgb` at 24% alpha.
- A hover state on the thumb that bumps the alpha to 40%.
- A short transition tied to `--duration-quick` and `--ease-transition`.

Apply `scrollbar-gutter` only on the page-level scroller that needs
shift-free behaviour — nested minimal-scrollbar regions will each reserve
their own 8px gutter if you don't.
