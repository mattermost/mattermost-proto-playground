Feature Discovery Panels surface capabilities only available on a higher-tier
SKU. They live in the System Console and are visible to admins — never to
end users. The goal is to communicate the value of the feature, not gate the
UI behind a paywall.

<Divider />

## Anatomy

A two-column card at Elevation 1 with a SKU tag, title, and description on
the left, and a supporting illustration on the right.

- <Num>1</Num> **Container** — surface at Elevation 1 with rounded corners.
- <Num>2</Num> **SKU tag** — Tag for the tier name (`PROFESSIONAL`,
  `ENTERPRISE`) in uppercase for scanability; keep it factual and short.
- <Num>3</Num> **Title** — what the feature does, in Metropolis 500.
- <Num>4</Num> **Description** — one or two sentences on the benefit.
- <Num>5</Num> **Primary CTA** — the next step (_Contact sales_, _Start
  trial_, _Upgrade_).
- <Num>6</Num> **Secondary CTA** — optional Tertiary button for _Learn
  more_ or supporting documentation.
- <Num>7</Num> **Illustration** — optional. Reinforces the topic without
  doing the explaining.

## Options

| Variant | Use when |
| ------- | -------- |
| Standard | A paid feature on a higher SKU. Primary CTA is _Contact sales_ or _Upgrade_. |
| Trial available | The admin can self-serve a trial. Primary CTA is _Start trial_, with a disclaimer. |
| No illustration | Tight or text-heavy areas of the System Console. |

<Divider />

## Behaviour

- **SKU tag visibility.** Hide the tag entirely when the promotion is not tier-specific.
- **Illustration.** Omit when layout budget is tight; lean on title and CTA hierarchy instead.

<Divider />

## Content

- **Sell the benefit, not the feature.** _Sync your AD/LDAP groups_ tells the
  admin what they get; _enable group syncing_ tells them what to click.
- **Be concise and scannable.** Most admins skim — title and CTA carry the
  weight.
- **Use action-oriented CTAs.** _Contact sales_, _Upgrade_, _Start trial_,
  _Learn more_.
- **Avoid fear or guilt.** Don't shame the admin for being on a lower tier —
  describe what unlocks when they upgrade.
