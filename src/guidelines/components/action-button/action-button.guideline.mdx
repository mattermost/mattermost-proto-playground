Action Buttons surface a small set of high-frequency actions in a fixed
context — a profile popover, a channel info panel, a card. They sit close to
the content they act on and read as a row of equal-weight choices.

Reach for an Action Button when an icon plus a one-word label communicates
faster than a labelled Button, and when several related actions need to sit
side-by-side without one outranking the others.

<Divider />

## Anatomy

Three parts — container, icon, and label — stacked vertically.

- <Num>1</Num> **Container** — the surface that gives the action its shape and
  hit target.
- <Num>2</Num> **Icon** — required. Reinforces the action at a glance.
- <Num>3</Num> **Label** — required. Action verb, four words or fewer.

## States

Action Buttons have three interactive states — Default, Hover, and Active —
plus a Destructive variant. The Active state signals that the action is
toggled on (e.g. "Following").

<SampleRow>
  <ActionButton icon={<MessageTextOutlineIcon size={20} />} label="Default" />
  <ActionButton icon={<AccountPlusOutlineIcon size={20} />} label="Active" active />
  <ActionButton icon={<MessageTextOutlineIcon size={20} />} label="Disabled" disabled />
</SampleRow>

<Divider />

## Destructive

Use the destructive variant only for actions that remove or undo something —
leaving a channel, removing a member from a group DM, deleting a saved item.
Pair it with a confirmation step; the button itself isn't a confirmation.

<SampleRow>
  <ActionButton
    icon={<TrashCanOutlineIcon size={20} />}
    label="Remove"
    destructive
  />
</SampleRow>
