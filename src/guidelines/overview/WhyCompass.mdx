Just as a compass points everyone in the same direction, a design system does
the same thing for a product and brand team. Since the Mattermost brand is
also embodied by the compass symbol, the Design System is called "Compass
Design System" — Mattermost's "true north" when it comes to design.

## What is a Design System?

A design system is a collection of guidelines, patterns, and components
supported by standards and documentation. It helps a team have a shared
understanding of the design approach and establishes a shared visual language
for products and communications.

<Divider />

## Why a Design System?

- Promotes consistency in design solutions
- Ensures products and communications have a cohesive look and feel
- Enables efficiency in designing and developing new features with reusable patterns
- Cuts down on design review times
- Saves time making global style changes
- Provides a shared language for the organization to talk about design
