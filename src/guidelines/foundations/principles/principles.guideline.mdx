Mattermost is a high-trust, enterprise messaging platform. The design
approach aims to represent trustworthiness in every interaction, unified
around three key principles: Fast, Obvious, and Forgiving.

## Fast

Every interaction with the Mattermost brand and product should feel
responsive. When a user clicks, taps, or types, immediate feedback is given.
Every interaction should be as quick as possible — from aiding in user
discovery, loading pages, sending messages, or receiving notifications —
saving users valuable time.

## Obvious

Everything — from features, interface layout, labeling, help text, and
product documentation — needs to be easy to find and understand. In the
interest of making core functionality apparent to users, distractions must be
minimized and superfluous elements omitted. Design patterns should be
familiar to the audience and leverage conventions that exist in other
familiar digital experiences. Functionality and design patterns should be
employed consistently to meet user expectations.

<Divider />

## Forgiving

Being forgiving means reducing or eliminating negative impact during failure
states such as: not losing data, nor deleting, updating, or sending something
the user didn't intend to. Wherever possible, preventative measures are taken
to prevent failure states. Features like confirmation dialogs on critical
actions or temporarily saving user input in case of disconnection are
examples of a forgiving recovery approach.
