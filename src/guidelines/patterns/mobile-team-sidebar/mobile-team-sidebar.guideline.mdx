The Mobile Team Sidebar is the 72px-wide strip for switching teams on phones.
It mirrors the desktop [Team Sidebar](/patterns/team-sidebar) — team avatars,
unread and mention badges, and Add team — and adds a Servers control above the
teams container for multi-server setups. The teams container uses the sidebar
header surface with a rounded top-right corner and sits below the device status
bar (the status area stays on the channel sidebar surface).

Use the Specimen tab for a live strip. Prefer desktop Team Sidebar on web.

## Anatomy

- <Num>1</Num> **Servers button** — Opens or switches the multi-server picker
  (affordance only in this prototype). Sits above the teams container, not inside
  the rounded header surface.
- <Num>2</Num> **Team avatars** — One `TeamAvatar` per team with Active outline,
  unread dot, or mention badge as needed. See [Team Avatar](/components/team-avatar).
- <Num>3</Num> **Add team** — Medium inverted icon button with plus, when adding
  teams is allowed.

## Behavior

Selecting a team should refresh channels and unreads for that team via
`onSelectTeam`. The active team’s avatar uses the Active state. Unread dots and
mention badges follow the same rules as desktop.

Tap targets are sized for touch; there is no hover chrome on team rows.
