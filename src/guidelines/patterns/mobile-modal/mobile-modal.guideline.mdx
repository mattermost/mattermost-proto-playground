The Mobile Modal is the nearly full-height iOS sheet for focused tasks such as
Settings. It pairs the
[Mobile Modal Navigation Bar](/patterns/mobile-modal-navigation-bar) with a
scrollable content slot for any body — menu lists, forms, or custom layouts.

This is distinct from the [Mobile Bottom Sheet](/patterns/mobile-bottom-sheet),
which is a shorter card with a drag handle for secondary actions.

Use the Specimen tab for a Settings example and a Child screen. For the full
device composition over a previous view, see the
[Mobile Modal](/layouts/mobile-modal) layout.

<Divider />

## Anatomy

The modal is composed of the following parts.

<MobileModalAnatomyStage />

- <Num>1</Num> **Modal Top Nav Bar** — Close (Parent) or Back (Child), title,
  optional subtitle or avatar, and optional trailing actions.
- <Num>2</Num> **Content slot** — Flexible body region. Scrolls independently
  under the fixed nav. Compose with [Mobile Menu Items](/components/mobile-menu-item)
  or any other content.

<Divider />

## Behavior

Keep the nav fixed while the body scrolls. Prefer Parent for the root modal and
Child when pushing nested screens inside the same sheet.

Backdrop, status-bar inset, and previous-view presentation are handled by
playground `MobileModalStage` — this pattern owns the sheet chrome and body
slot. The Specimen tab uses that stage for reference.

<Divider />

## Content

The body is intentionally unopinionated. Settings UIs typically use grouped
Mobile Menu Items; other flows can place forms, lists, or custom blocks in the
same slot.
