The Team Sidebar appears when a user belongs to more than one team on a
Mattermost server. It is the primary control for switching team context:
everything to the right of the strip (including the Channel Sidebar) updates to
match the selected team.

The strip is the leftmost column of the shell, uses the same dark sidebar header
background as the rest of the chrome, and stacks team identities vertically with
an Add team affordance at the bottom when enabled.

<Divider />

## Anatomy

The Team Sidebar is composed of Team Avatar entries (one per team) and an Add
team icon button.

<TeamSidebarAnatomyStage />

- <Num>1</Num> **Team avatars** — Each row is a `TeamAvatar` inside a hit target
  that selects that team. Teams may show an image, initials fallback, an Active
  outline state for the current team, an unread dot (`UnreadBadge`) when there is
  non-mention unread traffic, and a mention count badge when there are unread
  mentions. For anatomy, sizes, and fallback rules for the glyph itself, see the
  [Team Avatar](/components/team-avatar) component guidelines.
- <Num>2</Num> **Add team button** — A `Medium` inverted `IconButton` with a plus
  icon. Corner radius is tuned so the control lines up with team avatar rows and
  reads as part of the dark sidebar strip. Omit it when adding teams is not
  allowed.

<Divider />

## Behavior

When a team avatar is activated, the application should load that team’s data
and refresh the application in that team’s context — channels, unreads, and
drafts follow the selected team. Wire selection through `onSelectTeam` (and your
routing or store) as appropriate.

The current team should render its `TeamAvatar` in the Active state. When each
team carries unread or mention signals, expose them so the strip can show the
unread dot or mention count badge.
