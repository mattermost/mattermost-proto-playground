The Mobile Home layout is the primary Mattermost landing screen on phones —
team strip, channel list, and bottom tab bar inside an iPhone device frame.
System chrome (status bar, Dynamic Island, home indicator) belongs to the
device frame; sidebars and the tab bar pad their own safe areas.

Use the Specimen tab for the live composition. Other tab destinations are
covered by [Mobile Search](/layouts/mobile-search),
[Mobile Mentions](/layouts/mobile-mentions),
[Mobile Saved](/layouts/mobile-saved), and
[Mobile Profile](/layouts/mobile-profile). Conversation view is covered by
[Mobile Channel](/layouts/mobile-channel).

## Anatomy

- <Num>1</Num> **Device frame** — iPhone canvas with status bar and home
  indicator.
- <Num>2</Num> **Mobile Team Sidebar** — 72px strip for servers and teams. See
  [Mobile Team Sidebar](/patterns/mobile-team-sidebar).
- <Num>3</Num> **Mobile Channel Sidebar** — Team header, Find channels, and
  categorized rows. See
  [Mobile Channel Sidebar](/patterns/mobile-channel-sidebar) and
  [Mobile Channel Sidebar Item](/components/mobile-channel-sidebar-item).
- <Num>4</Num> **Mobile Tab Bar** — Home, Search, Mentions, Saved, and Profile.
  See [Mobile Tab Bar](/patterns/mobile-tab-bar).

<Divider />

## Behavior

Keep the team strip and tab bar fixed while the channel list scrolls. Tab
selection in this specimen is local UI state only — channel taps navigate to a
separate conversation screen (Mobile Channel), so the list does not show a
selected row. Prefer Large touch targets and Body 200 list labels throughout.

Compose with the `MobileHome` layout component, passing team sidebar, channel
sidebar, and tab bar slots.
